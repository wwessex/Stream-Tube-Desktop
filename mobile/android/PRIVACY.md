# Stream Tube for Android privacy notice

Stream Tube makes requests to YouTube when you browse or search, load thumbnails, or play videos. Google handles playback in the embedded YouTube player.

You can optionally connect a Google account. Google Play services handles account selection and permission. Stream Tube asks for read-only access to your YouTube account. It uses that access to display your YouTube channel name and subscriptions. Stream Tube keeps the access token in memory for the current session and sends it only to the YouTube Data API.

Stream Tube stores your saved videos, viewing history, recent searches, local subscriptions and settings on your device. Google account subscriptions are held in memory. Use Disconnect in Settings to remove the connection from Stream Tube. You can also revoke access in [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

When enabled in Settings, SponsorBlock and DeArrow receive a short hash of the video ID. The DeArrow thumbnail service and Return YouTube Dislike receive the public video ID.

The Android app uses the [YouTube API Services](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/api-services-terms-of-service). See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how Google processes requests.

Project contact [GitHub issues in the Android fork](https://github.com/ShiroKSH/Stream-Tube-Desktop/issues).
